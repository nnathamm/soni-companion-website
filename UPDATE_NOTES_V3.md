# Soni Website V3

## Visual direction

The homepage now uses an original product-first layout inspired by the restrained presentation of major technology product sites:

- black, white, and cool-gray palette
- Soni light blue used sparingly as an accent
- compact two-level navigation on the homepage
- centered product hero with a large Soni render
- full-bleed mission photography with overlaid copy
- large image-first progression tiles
- alternating white and black feature sections
- tighter, more minimal buttons and links

## Anonymous testimonials

A new homepage testimonials section is included. It intentionally contains no fabricated quotes. Three clearly labeled spaces are reserved for verified feedback from:

- an older adult participant
- a family member
- community staff

The section explains that feedback will be voluntary, reviewed, anonymous, and published without names, facilities, or identifying personal details.
